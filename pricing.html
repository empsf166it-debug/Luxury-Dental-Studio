Frequently Asked Questions

Is cosmetic dentistry covered by insurance?

Most purely cosmetic procedures (like veneers or teeth whitening) are not covered by dental insurance. However, if a procedure has a restorative necessity (such as a crown), a portion of the cost may be covered. We will help you navigate your benefits.

Can I cancel my membership at any time?

Our memberships require a minimum 12-month commitment to ensure you receive the full cycle of preventive care. After the first year, you can cancel with a 30-day notice.

Do I need a membership to book a consultation?

No, our clinic is open to all patients. Memberships are an optional benefit for those looking for structured, discounted ongoing care.

How do the EMI plans work?

We perform a soft credit check that does not affect your score. Based on the treatment cost, we can break down the payment into 6, 12, or 24 equal monthly installments with 0% interest.
